Get to know about us and why we write for you

Kaleido is a broad-interest magazine. We cover business, science, technology, culture, health and the everyday with the same question each time: what would a smart friend want to know about this?

▶

Our approach

Research first

Every claim has a source

We read the papers, the filings and the primary data before we write a single sentence.

Plain language

Written to be understood

No jargon for its own sake. If a 14-year-old can't follow it, the draft goes back.

Useful endings

Something you can act on

Each piece closes with what to do, try or watch next. Reading should change something.
