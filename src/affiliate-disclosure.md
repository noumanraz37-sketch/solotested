SoloTested is free to read. We pay for it in two ways:

1. **Affiliate links.** Some links to products are affiliate links. If you click one and buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
2. **Advertising.** Some pages may show ads.

## What this means for you

- Posts that contain affiliate links say so at the top of the post.
- Commissions never decide which products we recommend or the order we rank them in. If a tool is not good for a reader, we say so, whether or not it has an affiliate program.
- We often recommend free tools and tools with no affiliate program.
- Affiliate links on SoloTested are marked in the page code as sponsored links.
