## Who writes Kaleido

Kaleido is written and edited by [Nouman Raza](/author/nouman-raza/), a graphic designer and creative strategist who uses AI tools and automation in client work every day. Every guide carries a real byline.

## How we research

- We start from real questions people ask, on forums, in search and in our own work.
- Prices, plans and features are checked on the official product pages, and the date we checked is shown in the guide.
- Every statistic links to its original source.
- When we say we tested something, the guide shows what we did and what we saw.

## How we use AI

We use AI tools to help research topics, organize notes and draft sections. Every guide is then reviewed, fact-checked and edited by a person before it is published. Tests, opinions and experiences in our guides are real and our own. We never publish AI-invented quotes, statistics, reviews or experiences.

## Independence

Kaleido does not sell software. Some guides contain affiliate links, which pay us a commission if you buy (see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). Commissions never decide which tools we recommend or how we rank them, and we list real drawbacks for every tool we cover.

## Updates and corrections

Software changes fast. We revisit guides when prices or features change and show an "Updated" date when we do. If you spot a mistake, [tell us](/contact/) and we will fix it.
